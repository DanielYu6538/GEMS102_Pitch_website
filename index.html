The Research Gap

Current sensors like cameras and lidar work well in ideal conditions, but fail in weather situations such as fog and rain.

The Challenge

Weather conditions cause sensor data to become noisy with inaccurate readings. Research shows that removing "noisy" data causes to much data loss. Multisensor fusion has improved navigation in adverse weather conditions, but is not perfect.

Potential Approaches

  1. Prevent extreme data loss from denoising algorithms by combining it with multi-sensor fusion.
  2. Have comunication between multiple robots to share sensor data at longer ranges.

Research

  1. Dr. Pratap Tokekar (UMD) on data sharing between autonomous vehicles.
  2. Gupta, H., Lilienthal, A. J., & Andreasson, H. on denoising algorithms for autonomous vehicles.
  3. Palladin, E., Dietze, R., Narayanan, P., Bijelic, M., & Heide, F. on sensor fusion for autonomous vehicles.

Interdisciplinary Needs

This project requires diverse perspectives to solve complex technical and social problems.

Potential work in this project includes:

Computer Engineering / Computer Science

Work on ROS (Robot Operating System), software, and computer hardware. Work on navigation algorithms and sensor fusion models. Work on machine learning and AI.

Engineering / Science

Work on how signals are affected by rain and optimizing sensor hardware (Optics/Electrical Engineering).

Make sure the system is resilient to weather conditions.

Math / Statistics

Work on mathematical models for data validation and analyzing system performance.

Social Sci / Policy

Work with the community to ensure urban robots are designed for accessibility and community safety. Understand the impact of autonomous vehicles on the community.
